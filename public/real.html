    What is the Real Growth Score?

    Think of a bakery. The total value of bread on the shelf went up today. There are only two ways that can happen:

    →

    Each loaf sells for more

    People want the bread, so they pay a higher price. Same number of loaves, more value. That's real growth.

    ✓ Real 100
    →

    The baker bakes more loaves

    The price stays the same, but there's more bread on the shelf. The total looks bigger, yet nobody wants it any more than before.

    ⚠ New coins 0

    Crypto works the same way: market cap = price × number of coins.
    The Real Growth Score is the share of a coin's growth that came from the price (buyers), from 0 to 100.

    • ✓ Real 90–100Almost all of the growth came from buyers.
    • Mixed 50–89Mostly buyers, but some new coins were added.
    • ⚠ New coins 0–49Most of the growth came from new coins being released.
    • ⚠ Price fell 0The value rose only because of new coins. The price actually dropped.

    How the score is calculated. Market cap = price × circulating supply, so each day's change splits exactly into a price part and a supply part: (1 + market-cap change) = (1 + price change) × (1 + supply change). The score is the price part's share of the growth, measured on a log scale so the two shares add up to 100. Supply changes under 1% are treated as data noise. Shown only for coins that grew.
